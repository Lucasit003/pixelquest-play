# PixelQuest: The Thorn Kingdom

PixelQuest is a free pixel-art action RPG for children aged 7 to 12 that plays in the browser. Every power the hero uses is
earned by learning: a power opens only when the child has mastered the topics behind it and the hero has reached its level.
Gold, gear and experience points never unlock a power on their own.

**Play:** https://lucasit003.github.io/pixelquest-play/
**For parents and teachers:** https://lucasit003.github.io/pixelquest-play/parents.html

## What it teaches
Seven subjects (Math, Coding, Science, Geography, History, English and Money) across Elementary (grades 2 to 5, as a ladder),
Middle School (grades 6 to 8) and High School courses, including AP courses. Each topic has a lesson, a quiz, practice and a
mastery check. The parents page lists the current topic, question and course counts, generated from the game's data.
College has no lessons yet.

## Safety and privacy today
- No ads, no chat or messaging, no online play.
- No accounts or sign-in yet. Progress is saved on the device, in the browser's storage.
- No analytics or tracking: the game loads only its own files from this site.
- The grown-ups' report and links that leave the game sit behind a quick sum for grown-ups.
- This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs; see GitHub's privacy statement.

A full privacy policy will come before accounts open.

## How to play
- On a computer: Chrome or Safari, keyboard controls.
- On a phone: hold it sideways for touch controls.
- On iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open PixelQuest from its new icon for full screen.

This repository holds the built game only (a copy of `dist/`). Art and music credits are in the game (title screen, then Credits).
